ELIGIBILITY AND ADMISSION
UG: 10+2 with minimum 45-50% depending on programme; health sciences need Physics, Chemistry, Biology. B.Tech: 50% in Physics, Chemistry, Maths; age 17-24 on 31 Dec of the year. B.Tech lateral entry: 3-year engineering diploma, 50%, 6 seats, joins 2nd year. Pharm D: PCB or Maths, 50%, NEET or state test as per PCI. Admission is mainly merit-based; campus visit is optional and the process can be done online.
Process: enquiry, counselling, application form, registration fee, offer letter, admission counselling, enrolment fee, seat blocking, balance first-semester fee and marksheet verification, confirmation.

HOSTEL
Separate boys and girls blocks. Rooms are available for 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 persons sharing, in both AC and non-AC. Hostel fees are separate from tuition, about Rs 1.2 to 2.7 lakh per year, and the tariff includes mess food. Hostel deposit Rs 4,000 (refundable). Electricity deposit Rs 6,000; AC usage Rs 11 per unit. Laundry optional Rs 8,000 per academic year. Exact room-wise tariff is not published online; ask admissions.
