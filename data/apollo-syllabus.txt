APOLLO UNIVERSITY - SEMESTER-WISE SUBJECTS (source: official programme pages, apollouniversity.edu.in, collected Oct 2026)
Electives, MOOCs and non-credit activities are slots; subject names for elective options are on the programme page. Weekly contact hours for B.Tech CSE (AI&DS): Sem I-VIII = 21, 22, 22, 23, 21, 20, 20, 24 hrs/week. Periods per day and working days are not published.

== B.TECH CSE - COMMON FIRST THREE SEMESTERS (all B.Tech CSE specialisations) ==
Sem I: Engineering Physics, Engineering Mathematics, Problem Solving and Programming with C, Communicative English, University Elective I, Physics Lab, C Programming Lab.
Sem II: Probability & Statistics, Basic Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Data Structures, Python Programming, Environmental Studies, University Elective II, labs for BEEE, Data Structures, Python.
Sem III: Discrete Mathematics and Graph Theory, Design and Analysis of Algorithms, Object Oriented Programming through Java, Digital Logic Design, Health and Wellness (University Core III), University Elective III, Constitution of India, Java Lab, Digital Logic Lab.

== B.TECH CSE (AI & DATA SCIENCE) Sem IV-VIII ==
Sem IV: Computer Organization and Architecture, Management for Engineers, Foundations of Data Science, Database Management Systems, Operating Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Universal Human Values, DBMS Lab, Exploratory Data Analytics with R Lab.
Sem V: Machine Learning, Computer Networks, Automata and Compiler Design, Faculty Elective I, Programme Elective I, MOOC I, Entrepreneurship and Start-up Management, ML Lab, Networks Lab.
Sem VI: Natural Language Processing, Data Warehousing and Mining, Faculty Elective II, Programme Electives II-III, MOOC II, NLP Lab, Data Mining Lab.
Sem VII: Big Data Analytics, Neural Networks and Deep Learning, Programme Electives IV-V, MOOC III, Mini Project, Big Data Lab, Deep Learning Lab.
Sem VIII: Project Work, Internship evaluations.

== B.TECH CSE (AI & MACHINE LEARNING) Sem IV-VIII ==
Sem IV: Computer Organization and Architecture, Management for Engineers, Machine Learning, Database Management Systems, Operating Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Universal Human Values, DBMS Lab, Machine Learning Lab.
Sem V: Computer Networks, Automata and Compiler Design, Data Analysis and Visualization, Faculty Elective I, Programme Elective I, MOOC I, Entrepreneurship and Start-up Management, Networks Lab, Exploratory Data Analytics with R Lab.
Sem VI: Natural Language Processing, Advanced Machine Learning, Faculty Elective II, Programme Electives II-III (e.g. Explainable AI, Generative AI, Soft Computing), MOOC II, NLP Lab, Data Mining and ML Lab.
Sem VII: Big Data Analytics, Neural Networks and Deep Learning, Programme Electives IV-V (e.g. Reinforcement Learning, Autonomous Systems), MOOC III, Mini Project, Big Data Lab, Deep Learning Lab.
Sem VIII: Project Work, Internship evaluations.

== M.TECH (2 years) ==
M.Tech CSE: Sem I: Mathematical Foundations of Computer Science, Advanced Data Structures and Algorithms, Programme Elective I (Deep Learning / NLP / Generative AI), Programme Elective II (Enterprise Cloud Concepts / Data Science / NoSQL Databases), Research Methodology and IPR, Technical Seminar, Cloud Architecture, ADSA Lab.
